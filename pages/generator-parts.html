Accurate Fitment for Prime & Standby Diesel Generators

Industrial generator assemblies combine diesel prime movers, brushless alternators, electronic governors, and automatic voltage regulators—often customized by canopy builder and production batch. Even when two gensets share the same kVA rating, filter housings, diode bridges, and magnetic pickups can differ.

Verified Nameplate Cross-Referencing

To protect buyers from mismatched electrical or engine components, My Car Parts verifies every generator spare parts request against your engine serial number, alternator frame/AVR code, operating voltage (e.g., 400V 50 Hz or 480V 60 Hz), and existing part reference before confirming stock or quoting lead time.
